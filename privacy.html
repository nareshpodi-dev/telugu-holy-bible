Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Short version: the app does not collect, store or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your bookmarks, highlights, notes, reading progress, prayer journal, sermons and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. If you use the app's backup feature, the backup file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Location (Nearby churches)

Only when you tap Find churches near me does the app ask for your location. It is used once to look up churches around you on OpenStreetMap services (overpass-api.de and photon.komoot.io), which receive the approximate position in that request. The app does not store your location or send it anywhere else. The list of churches found is kept on your phone so it can be shown offline. You can refuse or withdraw location permission at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app works without it.

Notifications

Reminders (verse of the day, reading and prayer reminders) are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Reading aloud

Listening uses your phone's own text-to-speech voices. Some voices may be provided online by your phone's maker (Apple or Google) under their own privacy terms.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: use the support page.
