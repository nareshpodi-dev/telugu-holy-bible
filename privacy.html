Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Short version: the app does not collect, store or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Crash reports are sent only if you switch them on.

What stays on your phone

Your bookmarks, highlights, notes, reading progress, prayer journal, sermons and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. If you use the app's backup feature, the backup file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Location (Nearby churches)

Only when you tap Find churches near me does the app ask for your location. It is used once to look up churches around you on OpenStreetMap services (overpass-api.de and photon.komoot.io), which receive the approximate position in that request. The app does not store your location or send it anywhere else. The list of churches found is kept on your phone so it can be shown offline. You can refuse or withdraw location permission at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app works without it.

Notifications

Reminders (verse of the day, reading and prayer reminders) are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Reading aloud

Listening uses your phone's own text-to-speech voices. Some voices may be provided online by your phone's maker (Apple or Google) under their own privacy terms.

Voice search

When you tap the microphone on Search or Go To, the app asks for microphone (and on iPhone, speech recognition) permission and listens until you stop speaking. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's speech service — Apple on iPhone and iPad, Google (or your phone maker) on Android — under their own privacy terms. The app receives only the recognised words, uses them for that search, and does not record, store or send your voice anywhere else. You can turn microphone access off at any time in your phone's settings.

Crash reports (optional, off unless you turn them on)

In Settings you can turn on Send crash reports. Only then, if something goes wrong in the app, a report of the error is sent to Sentry (sentry.io), the service we use to find and fix bugs: the error message, where in the app's code it happened, the app version and the kind of phone and system. It never includes your name, notes, bookmarks, prayers, location or anything you have written, and no screenshots. Reports are not used for advertising or tracking. Turn the switch off to stop sending them.

Shared verse links

Verses you share include a link to a page on our website that shows the verse. Opening that page loads the verse from the same website; the website does not use cookies or tracking.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
